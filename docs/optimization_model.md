# HimKavach Optimization Mathematical Formulation

## 1. Tactical Scenario-based MPC (MILP)

### 1.1 Sets and Indices
- $t \in \mathcal{T} = \{1, \dots, T\}$: Time grid steps ($T = 90$, 24 steps @ 15 min, 66 steps @ 60 min).
- $\Delta t_t$: Duration of step $t$ in hours ($\Delta t_t = 0.25$ for $t \le 24$, $\Delta t_t = 1.0$ for $t > 24$).
- $s \in \mathcal{S} = \{1, \dots, K\}$: Scenario set ($K \approx 15$ copula scenarios plus stress scenario).
- $\pi_s$: Probability of scenario $s$ with $\sum_{s \in \mathcal{S}} \pi_s = 1$.
- $g \in \mathcal{G} = \{1, \dots, N_g\}$: Diesel generators.
- $k \in \mathcal{K} = \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$: Load priority tiers (0: Life Support, 1: Protected Science, 2: Comfort/Ops, 3: Deferrable).
- $b \in \mathcal{B} = \{1, \dots, N_b\}$: Battery degradation depth bands.

### 1.2 First-Stage Decision Variables (Non-anticipative, Shared across Scenarios)
- $u_{g, t} \in \{0, 1\}$: Commitment status of generator $g$ at step $t$ ($1 = \text{ON}$, $0 = \text{OFF}$).
- $v_{g, t} \in \{0, 1\}$: Start-up event of generator $g$ at step $t$.
- $w_{g, t} \in \{0, 1\}$: Shut-down event of generator $g$ at step $t$.

### 1.3 Second-Stage Decision Variables (Scenario-Specific Recourse)
- $P_{g, s, t} \ge 0$: Electrical output of generator $g$ in scenario $s$ at step $t$ (kW).
- $F_{g, s, t} \ge 0$: Fuel consumption rate of generator $g$ in scenario $s$ at step $t$ (L/h).
- $P_{bat, s, t}^{ch} \ge 0, P_{bat, s, t}^{dis} \ge 0$: Battery charge and discharge power (kW).
- $E_{bat, s, t} \ge 0$: Battery stored energy at end of step $t$ (kWh).
- $P_{wind, s, t}^{curt} \ge 0, P_{pv, s, t}^{curt} \ge 0$: Renewable curtailment power (kW).
- $S_{k, s, t} \ge 0$: Unserved (shed) power for load tier $k$ (kW).
- $P_{def, s, t} \ge 0$: Power allocated to deferrable processes / snow melting (kW).
- $T_{in, s, t}$: Indoor building temperature (°C).
- $Q_{heat, s, t} \ge 0$: Total thermal heating delivered to building (kW_th).
- $Q_{aux, s, t} \ge 0$: Electric resistance auxiliary heating power (kW_e).
- $\zeta$: Value-at-Risk threshold for Rockafellar–Uryasev CVaR formulation.
- $z_s \ge 0$: Scenario shortfall above $\zeta$ in CVaR formulation.
- $\sigma_{s, t} \ge 0$: Slack on dynamic reserve violation (kWh).
- $\xi_{s, t} \ge 0$: Slack on Tier 0 load shedding (kWh), with astronomical penalty.

### 1.4 Objective Function
$$\begin{aligned}
\min_{\mathbf{u}, \mathbf{v}, \mathbf{w}, \mathbf{x}_s} \quad
& \sum_{s \in \mathcal{S}} \pi_s \sum_{t \in \mathcal{T}} \Delta t_t \Big[ (c_{fuel} + \lambda_{fuel}) \sum_{g} F_{g, s, t} + c_{deg} P_{bat, s, t}^{dis} + \sum_{k} \text{VoLL}_k S_{k, s, t} + c_{slack} \sigma_{s, t} + M \xi_{s, t} \Big] \\
& + \sum_{t \in \mathcal{T}} \sum_{g} c_{start, g} v_{g, t} \\
& + \beta \left( \zeta + \frac{1}{1 - \alpha} \sum_{s \in \mathcal{S}} \pi_s z_s \right) \\
& - \gamma_{term} \sum_{s \in \mathcal{S}} \pi_s E_{bat, s, T}
\end{aligned}$$

Where:
- $c_{fuel}$: Baseline fuel cost ($/L).
- $\lambda_{fuel}$: Dynamic fuel shadow price generated by the daily Fuel Glide Path LP ($/L).
- $c_{start, g}$: Generator wear and crank-fuel cost per startup ($).
- $\text{VoLL}_k$: Value of Lost Load for tier $k$ ($\text{VoLL}_0 \gg \text{VoLL}_1 \gg \text{VoLL}_2 \gg \text{VoLL}_3$).
- $\beta$: CVaR risk aversion weight; $\alpha$: CVaR confidence level (e.g. 0.95).
- $\gamma_{term}$: Battery terminal value coefficient preventing artificial depletion at the horizon boundary.

### 1.5 Constraints

#### 1.5.1 Generator Operational Constraints
$$\begin{aligned}
v_{g, t} - w_{g, t} &= u_{g, t} - u_{g, t-1}, \quad \forall g, t \\
\sum_{\tau = t - UT_g + 1}^{t} v_{g, \tau} &\le u_{g, t}, \quad \forall g, t \ge UT_g \quad (\text{Min Up-time}) \\
\sum_{\tau = t - DT_g + 1}^{t} w_{g, \tau} &\le 1 - u_{g, t}, \quad \forall g, t \ge DT_g \quad (\text{Min Down-time}) \\
P_{min, g} u_{g, t} &\le P_{g, s, t} \le P_{max, g} u_{g, t}, \quad \forall g, s, t \\
P_{g, s, t} - P_{g, s, t-1} &\le R_{up, g} \Delta t_t + P_{min, g} v_{g, t}, \quad \forall g, s, t \quad (\text{Ramp Up}) \\
P_{g, s, t-1} - P_{g, s, t} &\le R_{down, g} \Delta t_t + P_{min, g} w_{g, t}, \quad \forall g, s, t \quad (\text{Ramp Down}) \\
F_{g, s, t} &= a_g u_{g, t} + b_g P_{g, s, t}, \quad \forall g, s, t \quad (\text{Linear Fuel Curve with No-load Offset})
\end{aligned}$$

#### 1.5.2 Electrical Power Balance
For all scenarios $s$ and steps $t$:
$$\begin{aligned}
\sum_{g \in \mathcal{G}} P_{g, s, t} + (P_{wind, s, t}^{avail} - P_{wind, s, t}^{curt}) + (P_{pv, s, t}^{avail} - P_{pv, s, t}^{curt}) + P_{bat, s, t}^{dis} - P_{bat, s, t}^{ch} \\
= \sum_{k=0}^{2} (D_{k, s, t} - S_{k, s, t}) + P_{def, s, t} + Q_{aux, s, t} + P_{bat, heat, s, t}
\end{aligned}$$
where $S_{0, s, t} \le \xi_{s, t}$ (Tier 0 shed permitted only via high-penalty slack $\xi$).

#### 1.5.3 Battery Storage Dynamics and Limits
$$\begin{aligned}
E_{bat, s, t} &= E_{bat, s, t-1} + \left( \eta_{ch} P_{bat, s, t}^{ch} - \frac{1}{\eta_{dis}} P_{bat, s, t}^{dis} \right) \Delta t_t, \quad \forall s, t \\
SoC_{min} E_{cap} &\le E_{bat, s, t} \le SoC_{max} E_{cap} \\
0 &\le P_{bat, s, t}^{ch} \le P_{max, ch}(T_{bat, s, t}) \\
0 &\le P_{bat, s, t}^{dis} \le P_{max, dis}(T_{bat, s, t}) \\
E_{bat, s, t} &\ge E_{reserve, s, t} - \sigma_{s, t}, \quad \forall s, t \quad (\text{Dynamic Reserve Constraint})
\end{aligned}$$

#### 1.5.4 Dynamic Reserve Specification
$$E_{reserve, s, t} = E_{bridge}(t) + E_{uncertainty, s, t}$$
where:
- $E_{bridge}(t) = (D_{0, t} + D_{1, t}) \times t_{genset\_start}$: Energy required to bridge critical loads during generator start-up/failover under cold ambient conditions.
- $E_{uncertainty, s, t}$: Reserve margin based on forecast net load variance and storm onset probability.

#### 1.5.5 N-1 Firm Capacity Constraint
At every step $t$, the remaining committed generators with the single largest online unit failing ($g^* = \arg\max_g P_{max, g} u_{g, t}$) must cover Tier 0 and Tier 1 peak demand with zero renewable contribution:
$$\sum_{g \in \mathcal{G}} P_{max, g} u_{g, t} - \max_{g \in \mathcal{G}} (P_{max, g} u_{g, t}) + P_{max, dis} \ge D_{0, t} + D_{1, t}, \quad \forall t$$
*(Linearized via standard auxiliary variable $\mu_t \ge P_{max, g} u_{g, t}, \forall g$).*

#### 1.5.6 Building Thermal Model (RC Dynamics)
$$\begin{aligned}
T_{in, s, t} &= T_{in, s, t-1} + \frac{\Delta t_t}{C_{th}} \left[ \frac{T_{amb, s, t} - T_{in, s, t-1}}{R_{th}} + Q_{heat, s, t} + Q_{internal, t} \right] \\
Q_{heat, s, t} &= \sum_{g \in \mathcal{G}} \eta_{rec} \cdot \text{LHV}_{fuel} \cdot F_{g, s, t} + Q_{aux, s, t} \\
T_{in}^{min} &\le T_{in, s, t} \le T_{in}^{max} \quad (\text{Permits thermal pre-heating before storm onset})
\end{aligned}$$

#### 1.5.7 Rockafellar–Uryasev CVaR Linearization
For each scenario $s \in \mathcal{S}$:
$$z_s \ge \sum_{t \in \mathcal{T}} \Delta t_t \left( \sum_{k} \text{VoLL}_k S_{k, s, t} + c_{slack} \sigma_{s, t} \right) - \zeta, \quad z_s \ge 0$$

## 2. Strategic Fuel Glide Path (Daily LP)

The glide path tracks the annual diesel envelope:
$$\min \sum_{d = 1}^{D_{resupply}} c_d F_{day, d}$$
subject to:
$$\begin{aligned}
Fuel(d) &= Fuel(d-1) - F_{day, d} \\
Fuel(d) &\ge F_{min}(d) = (D_{resupply} - d + D_{contingency}) \times \bar{D}_{crit} \times \bar{F}_{rate} \\
Fuel(0) &= Fuel_{current}
\end{aligned}$$
The dual variable on the current inventory balance constraint yields the fuel shadow price $\lambda_{fuel}$.
